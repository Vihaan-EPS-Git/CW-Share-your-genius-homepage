For those who are too lazy to learn a little about fouls, here's a fancy overview:

In the noble sport of basketball, a foul constitutes an illicit manifestation of physical contact or a egregious departure from chivalrous sportsmanship that violates the established decrees of the realm. These transgressions predominantly encompass tactile infractions such as striking, shoving, or obstruction, alongside non-tactile technical offenses like insolent disputation with the arbiters. Consequently, the aggrieved party is frequently endowed with possession of the leather orb or granted uncontested free throw opportunities from the charity stripe. Furthermore, should an individual repeatedly incur these demerits, they shall inevitably forfeit their eligibility and suffer banishment from the remainder of the contest.
